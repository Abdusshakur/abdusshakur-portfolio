02 / THE ENGINEERING METHOD

Many parts.
One system.

From a sensor's response to a backend's reliability, I enjoy the questions that appear when components become connected systems.

01

Observe & measure

Understand the operating environment, gather evidence and identify constraints before building.

02

Design the interfaces

Think beyond isolated parts: hardware, data, control logic and real users have to work together.

03

Build & investigate

Prototype, debug, test assumptions and interrogate what the results actually mean.

04

Make the work legible

Document the decisions, communicate trade-offs and share what others can learn from.
