Who I am

I'm a Mechatronics Engineering graduate from the Federal University of Technology, Minna. My professional experience spans industrial automation, technical investigations, software development, applied machine learning and research.

I also have an earlier background in journalism and community-focused research, which developed my ability to investigate problems, challenge assumptions, communicate clearly and consider the wider context of technical decisions.

My journey

Early in university, I worked in campus journalism and researched emerging technology in Africa. I later gained practical exposure to industrial controls, embedded computing and mechatronics projects. More recently, I've worked with production software systems, transaction data, Python automation and complex technical investigations.

These experiences were not a single straight line. Together they helped me see why reliable engineering depends on understanding both individual components and the systems around them.

How I think about engineering

Understand the real problem. Examine the evidence. Design for the complete system, including the constraints and people involved.

I enjoy projects that demand both technical depth and cross-disciplinary thinking—connecting physical hardware, software services, data and intelligent decision-making.

Where I'm going

My career direction is toward Intelligent Systems Engineering and, in the longer term, Systems Architecture. I want to develop the judgment and practical expertise to design, integrate and lead the development of complex engineered systems.

I'm particularly interested in energy and industrial applications, but I aim to keep my skills transferable across robotics, digital infrastructure, aerospace and other domains.
