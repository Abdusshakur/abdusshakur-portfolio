Engineering context

Industrial automation connects control logic, electrical power, sensors, actuators, and equipment safety. During an internship with Greenpeg Engineering, I gained practical exposure to those interfaces in real engineering work.

Motor control & panel work

Contributed to work involving a 75 kW motor control panel, including panel-related tasks and electrical integrity checks. This experience reinforced the importance of careful wiring, verification, documentation, and safe commissioning procedures.

PLC & drive configuration

Worked with Siemens S7-1200 PLCs and variable-frequency drives (VFDs), learning how control commands and motor-drive parameters affect physical equipment.

System verification

Participated in testing and engineering workflows involving component checks and power-related assessments. In industrial settings, correctness involves electrical safety and reliability as well as functioning software.

What I took forward

This work grounded my software and intelligent-systems interests in physical-system constraints: control behaviour, equipment interfaces, electrical integrity, and the operational consequences of design choices.
